White Water Rafting Co.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is simple: to get people out on the river, safely and joyfully. We believe the best conversations happen between rapids, that teamwork is learned with a paddle in hand, and that everyone deserves one truly wild day on the water. Our mission is to deliver unforgettable adventures while protecting the rivers we love.

History

White Water Rafting Co. began in 2014 when a handful of Gulu river guides pooled their savings and bought a single raft. Back then, the Nile's rapids belonged almost entirely to visiting expeditions — local families had no way onto the water. That first season carried forty guests down the river. By the second, word had spread far enough that we needed a second raft, and then a third.

Today our guides run three scheduled trips a day, from the gentle Family Float to the Full-Day Extreme Challenge, plus overnight camping on the riverbank. Sarah Namono leads a team of certified guides, and every trip still includes gear, safety briefings, and river permits. The fleet has grown and the guest list has grown with it, but the promise from that first season hasn't changed: small groups, local guides, and a day on the water you'll remember.
